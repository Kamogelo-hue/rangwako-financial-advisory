WHO WE ARE

Your partner in financial organisation and compliance

Rangwako Financial Advisory (RFA) is a locally owned Level 1 BBBEEE company with 100 percent black ownership. RFA was formed as a company committed to supplying goods & services for the mining industry and Plant, Equipment and Transportation services.

The company’s management philosophy is based on responsibility and mutual respect, while encouraging ownership of responsibilities to help foster personal and professional growth. RFA focuses on productivity for growth and profitability, and respect for its customers and employees to increase repeat and referral business while minimizing employee turnover.

Additionally, the company encourages employees to have fun by allowing creative independence and providing professional challenges that are realistic, attainable, and rewarding. We have technology which allows our products to work with all existing systems that is currently in operation. As our products and systems can be customized for the end user for their unique, individual applications.
